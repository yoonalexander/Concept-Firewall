# Bounded M3 spatial follow-up

Run `20261023T122529782263Z-m3-followup`; complete provenance in `results/manifests/20261023T122529782263Z-m3-followup.json`.

Exploratory development-only evaluation on 1600 puzzles (226 failures). All 400 original test identities were excluded from feature capture and fitting. No fresh test result is claimed.

Exactly two frozen spatial variants were evaluated: row means projected to 256 dimensions and all cell-position latents projected to 256 dimensions. Projection signs/seeds are fixed without labels. Eight row-separator positions are excluded from spatial variants; the original 256-dimensional global mean is retained as a baseline. Every reconstructed current board and global mean matches the audited parent data.

Four grouped outer folds assess the method. Two grouped inner folds select regularization and the cheap baseline family using log loss. Scaling fits only inner/outer training rows. Cheap-family selection never uses its outer assessment labels. Limited digit-renaming/transpose equivalence is grouped; full Sudoku symmetry equivalence remains unaudited.

AUROC below is the sample-weighted mean of outer-fold AUROCs; pooled OOF AUROC is secondary in the manifest. New discrimination uses raw margins, without a calibration fit or sign inversion. Brier scores use uncalibrated logistic probabilities. This is exploratory reuse of exposed data; cross-validation does not restore fresh confirmation.

## Decision and limits

Candidate criteria were fixed in `configs/m3_followup.json`: AUROC at least 0.65, lift at least 0.03 over both selected cheap and mean-addition controls, positive lift in at least three folds, and paired interval lower bound above zero. Primary 97.5% intervals account for two declared primary spatial comparisons by a Bonferroni-style coverage choice; secondary-level results are exploratory. Bootstrap resamples groups within outer assessment folds and does not refit models, so it does not capture all fitting/selection uncertainty.

The checked official hard-Sudoku release has only the same puzzle and solution files already evaluated. A resplit, digit renaming, or transpose does not create fresh confirmation. No paid compute, new foundation model, branching, policy controller, natural-language extension or viewer was used. Later milestones remain gated regardless of exploratory scores.

Offline prefix capture took 6.5 seconds; CPU fitting/evaluation took 23.7 seconds. These are experimental job costs, not an online speedup or monitoring-overhead comparison. Original protected artifacts were unchanged.
